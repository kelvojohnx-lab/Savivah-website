How vibe coding works here

From a sentence to a shipped site

"Vibe coding" means describing what you want in plain language and watching AI tools generate real, working code from it. Here, that speed is the starting point — every screen still gets a human engineering pass before it goes live.

01

Describe

You tell me what the site needs to do and feel like — in plain language, references, or a rough sketch.

02

Generate

AI-assisted tools scaffold layout, copy, and components fast, so we're looking at something real within hours.

03

Refine

Every generated screen gets a human pass — accessibility, performance, and your brand details, tightened by hand.

04

Ship

Your site goes live on your domain, connected to the tools you already use — forms, payments, analytics, CMS.
